HOW TO PLAY

The Great Lexical Collapse

1. The Shrinking Words:
You start with a gargantuan word (up to 35 letters long!). Each time you spell a word correctly, the next word is exactly 1 letter shorter, until you reach a solitary 1-letter word!
2. The 5-Minute Master Clock:
There is a single timer that does not reset when you get a word right. You must type all words before the 5 minutes (300 seconds) run out!
3. The Shrinking Chamber:
With every word solved, the hydraulic compactor chamber physically squeezes inward. Can your fingers handle the pressure?
4. Controls:
Simply use your physical keyboard to type the highlighted next letter! Press ESC to pause at any time.
